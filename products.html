Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Here's what you'll usually find in our case. Prices change a little with the seasons. We don't sell online, but you can send us a pre-order request and pick it up in the shop.

Breads

Sourdough, country white, whole wheat, rye, and seeded loaves. Baked every morning.

Price range: $5 to $11 per loaf

Pastries

Croissants, cinnamon rolls, fruit danishes, scones, muffins, and cookies. Great for a commute or a coffee break.

Price range: $2.50 to $5 each

Cakes

Layer cakes, sheet cakes, and cupcakes for birthdays, showers, and office parties. Custom cakes need at least 72 hours' notice.

Price range: $28 to $85 per cake, or $3.50 per cupcake

Catering and Event Trays

Mixed pastry and cookie trays for meetings and family events. Serves 12 to 40 people.

Price range: $35 to $120 per tray
