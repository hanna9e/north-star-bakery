About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star started in 2014 with one oven and a sourdough starter that Maria Okafor's grandmother kept going for decades. It was supposed to be a weekend market stand. The line kept getting longer, so a year later we moved into our spot on Maple Street, and we've been here ever since.

Behind the Scenes

Where Our Ingredients Come From

We buy flour from a regional mill and get eggs and dairy from farms within about 60 miles. Fruit fillings follow the season, so the danishes change a few times a year. If an ingredient doesn't taste right, we don't bake with it.
